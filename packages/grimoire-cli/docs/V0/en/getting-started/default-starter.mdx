# Default Starters

To provide total flexibility, Grimoire relies on two fundamental starters. These tools are the cornerstones of the framework: they allow you to transition from a simple user to an ecosystem creator.

## Create a custom CLI with "custom-cli"

The **custom-cli** starter is designed for those who wish to build their own personalized command-line interface. Much more than a simple template, it automates all the necessary structure to:

*   **Develop** your own command logic.
*   **Share** your tool easily via NPM.
*   **Empower** your end users to extend your CLI themselves thanks to the native management of the local `.cli` folder.

It is the ideal tool for standardizing processes within your organization.

## Share resources with "custom-tome"

If your goal is to distribute reusable building blocks, the **custom-tome** starter is your entry point. A "Tome" is a specialized NPM package that allows you to:

*   **Group** complex code generators.
*   **Distribute** entire framework configurations.
*   **Modularize** your tools so that each team can pick only what they need.

Together, **custom-cli** and **custom-tome** form the engine that makes Grimoire truly extensible and collaborative.
